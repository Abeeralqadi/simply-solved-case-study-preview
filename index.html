How a Dubai healthcare provider addressed overdue VAT returns—and strengthened the finance process behind them.

An FTA request uncovered more than a filing issue. Simply Solved dealt with the immediate VAT requirement, then addressed the reporting and systems weaknesses making finance harder to manage.

01

The Need

The client needed to respond to overdue VAT returns. The work also revealed a wider need for clearer, faster financial reporting.

In January 2019, the Federal Tax Authority contacted the provider about its 2018 VAT returns. Because its services were charged at 0%, the business had assumed that regular returns were not required. Returns were outstanding and late-filing penalties had accumulated.

The consultation also exposed pressure elsewhere. Management could not get a dependable view of overdue insurer balances, the P&L took 18 days after month-end to complete, and limited VAT knowledge made the finance process harder to control.
